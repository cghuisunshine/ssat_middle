# SSAT Middle Level practice

Open [the contents page](content.html) or [vocabulary practice](ssat_middle_vocabulary.html) directly in a browser. The practice page is self-contained and works offline; reviews, stars, and custom words stay in browser storage.

Open [Middle Level math practice](ssat_middle_math.html) for 150 scored quantitative questions from Ivy Global, Test Ninjas, and Kaplan. Each has a worked solution and strategy tip; 35 self-contained diagrams preserve the necessary figures and charts. Six timed sets contain exactly 25 questions and last 30 minutes each, following one quantitative section of the [official Middle Level format](https://www.admission.org/help/whats-on-the-middle-level-ssat). Timed practice locks navigation to its section, starts with blank scratch space, hides solutions and reference help until submission, and preserves the deadline on reload. Guided practice includes topic and progress filters, flags, a math reference, and saved scratch work. Answers were independently solved and compared with publisher keys. Documented wording clarifications appear with the affected solutions, including a repaired Test Ninjas distractor. Upper Level, ISEE, experimental, and sample questions are excluded.

Math data lives in `tools/math-data-*.json`, with UI sources in `tools/math-page.*`. Run `python3 tools/build_math_page.py` to rebuild the standalone page and `node tools/check_math.cjs` for its format, source-key, arithmetic, diagram, and offline checks. `tools/check_math_browser.js` runs in an isolated math-page browser context to verify all 150 questions, six timed sections, scratch notes, navigation, expiry, and backup restoration. The page embeds all questions, worked solutions, and SVGs and needs no external assets or runtime network connection. Progress and scratch work stay in browser storage, with JSON backup and restore.

Open [Middle Level writing practice](ssat_middle_writing.html) for 20 prompts, guided planning, an autosaving writing editor, and a self-review checklist. Seven 25-minute timed pairs offer one story starter and one personal prompt; students choose one after the timer starts. The deadline survives reloads, the editor locks when time expires, and revisions are saved separately from finished originals. Writing is unscored. Drafts can be downloaded as text, printed, or backed up to JSON; merging a backup keeps existing drafts. Eight prompts come from the explicitly Middle Level writing pages in Ivy Global, Princeton Review, Kaplan, and Test Ninjas. Twelve are original practice prompts. Older two-story source pairs are used for guided practice; timed pairs follow the current [official Middle Level format](https://www.admission.org/help/whats-on-the-middle-level-ssat). Upper Level, ISEE, and argumentative essay drills are excluded.

Writing source data is in `tools/writing-data.json`, with UI sources in `tools/writing-page.*`. Run `python3 tools/build_writing_page.py` to rebuild the standalone page and `node tools/check_writing.cjs` for its source, format, and offline checks. `tools/check_writing_browser.js` runs in an isolated writing-page browser context to verify planners, drafting, all seven timed pairs, expiry, separate revisions, safe printing, and backup merging. The page embeds the shared Wordwise styles plus writing-specific styles and needs no network connection to practice.

Open [Middle Level reading practice](ssat_middle_reading.html) for 37 passages and 182 questions selected from `reading_materials/`. Guided practice includes immediate explanations; four sets of exactly seven passages and 40 questions support 40-minute practice with answers hidden until submission. Passage notes, bookmarks, latest results, and backup/restore work locally in your browser. No external assets or services are needed to practice.

Timed practice offers the complete seven-passage Kaplan test and three mixed-source sets. Every new set contains seven complete passages, exactly 40 five-choice questions, and all five genres. The passage list, top counters, navigation, scoring, and review are restricted to the selected set. Question numbers run 1–40. Reloading keeps the same set, answers, and deadline; existing seven-passage sessions resume, while answers from older eight-passage sessions move back to guided practice. Guided practice retains all 37 passages. The [official SSAT outline](https://www.admission.org/help/whats-on-the-middle-level-ssat) specifies 40 questions in 40 minutes; [Kaplan’s outline](https://www.kaptest.com/ssat/what-is-the-ssat) describes seven passages.

The reading bank includes only explicitly labeled SSAT Middle Level sections from Ivy Global, Test Ninjas, Kaplan, Princeton Review, and GoTestPrep. It excludes SSAT Upper Level, all ISEE, mixed-level drills, other subjects, experimental sections, download listings, and the duplicated Ivy full-test reading section. Kaplan contributes only PDF pages 462–473. Publisher keys verify the Ivy, Test Ninjas, Kaplan, and GoTestPrep answers. The Princeton excerpt has no key; its answers were prepared from the passages and are labeled accordingly. Its question 9 has one clarified choice; other source-reference cleanup is documented in the page.

Reviewed reading data lives in `tools/reading-data-*.json`; UI sources are `tools/reading-page.*`. Rebuild the standalone page with `python3 tools/build_reading_page.py` and check its data, publisher keys, and offline structure with `node tools/check_reading.cjs`. `tools/check_reading_browser.js` runs in an open reading-page browser context to verify all 182 questions, four seven-passage timed sets, grading, notes, and deadline behavior; it restores the original saved browser state afterward. Source texts remain credited to their publishers; keep this collection for local study.

The expanded bank contains 1,100 words with context sentences, 47 additional meanings, 45 groups of related words, and 85 analogy questions. The latest expansion adds 150 words and seven meanings from all 37 reviewed Middle Level passages in `reading_materials/`, spanning Ivy Global, Test Ninjas, Kaplan, Princeton Review, and GoTestPrep. It includes every headword in the two Ivy Global sample lists checked in the [coverage audit](vocabulary_coverage_audit.md). These are preparation benchmarks, not an official exhaustive SSAT word list.

**Check answer** immediately shows a prebuilt lesson for the exact word meaning or analogy relationship, a comparison of every displayed choice, a memory cue, and Chinese support when enabled. All 1,147 word senses and 85 analogies have lessons embedded in the HTML. The page uses no AI endpoint or network requests to explain answers.

The lessons also appear after a skipped question and in the results of ordinary and timed drills. They never change grades or review records. Word lessons are compiled ahead of time from the definitions, examples, related-word groups, and teaching notes in `tools/word-lesson-notes.txt`. Each analogy has authored distractor explanations in `tools/analogy-choice-notes.txt`. Randomized quizzes combine those existing lessons locally to match the choices actually shown. Learner-added words use their supplied definitions locally.

Expansion source files are in `tools/`. Definitions, translations, and sentences are original practice content. `vocabulary-benchmarks.json` records headwords from the publisher's [75-word core sample](https://downloads.ivyglobal.com/ssat/segments/English_75_Core_SSAT_Words.pdf) and [125-word essential sample](https://downloads.ivyglobal.com/ssat/SSAT_ISEE_Vocabulary_1.pdf).

`tools/reading-vocabulary.json` contains the reading-derived entries, original teaching notes and memory cues, and source passage IDs with the exact inflected forms found in those passages. Word details and flashcards show each reading connection, including the source filename and PDF pages where applicable. The builder checks the forms against the reviewed `tools/reading-data-*.json` texts, so rebuilding does not require the ignored source downloads. New words use their base forms where appropriate: for example, “botched” becomes **botch**, and “figures” becomes **figure**. Distinct senses, such as a shop **counter** and the verb **counter**, retain separate examples and lessons.

After changing the expansion sources, rebuild the embedded data and UI, then run the data and behavior checks:

```sh
python3 tools/update_vocabulary.py
node tools/check_vocabulary.cjs
node tools/check_prebuilt_explanations.cjs
```

The builder preserves existing word IDs, the browser storage key, and the first 60 analogy IDs. It updates the contents-page counts and requires a context sentence for every built-in word. No build tools are needed to use the finished HTML page.

The explanation checks run with offline browser stubs and a network-request trap. They verify all 4,588 word-question variants, every analogy and its choices, exact word senses, immediate feedback, timed-review explanations, safe text rendering, Chinese toggling, and grade preservation. The vocabulary checks also verify every reading-derived entry and source connection and preserve progress for formerly custom words that are now built in.
